## Findings

1. NEED: 2 of 4 built. Built when defined as criterion-relative necessity (LLM 1, LLM 2; XPN's own reading). Leaks when tied to persistence or regulation, where coders then ask why persistence counts (LLM 4: teleology).
2. WANT, KNOW and SAY leak for all four coders, including the neutral coder, even with P.
3. For WANT all four name one family: the P must be the thing's own, matter to it, or be preferred by it. LLM 4 frames the whole gap more broadly as normativity / teleology / intentionality.
4. "P held inside X" is refuted as the want/need difference: every counter-case has a located P.
5. P helps everywhere but only partly. LLM 4: P "helped, but only as a placeholder for normativity, not as an analysis of it" — the same gap XPN leaves open (A3–A4 treat P as given).
6. KNOW: LLM 4 names justification (non-accidental correctness, Gettier), not aboutness. A second, distinct gap for KNOW.
7. LIVE: 1 of 4 built (LLM 1, via self-produced boundary).

Caveats: LLM 1 was not blind. LLM 4 used disallowed vocabulary in its definitions. All coders are LLMs.
